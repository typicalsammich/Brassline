At 1st Choice Plumbing, our story begins with family, hard work, and the goal of building a better future. We are a family-owned plumbing company founded on the belief that honest work, dependable service, and treating people right are the foundation of a business that lasts.

For Josue, plumbing was more than just a job. With over 10 years of hands-on plumbing experience, he spent years learning the trade, working with customers, and understanding what it takes to get the job done. Throughout those years, he saw the value of the work he was providing to his clients. He knew he could take the experience he had gained and build something of his own, something that would allow him to take greater ownership of his work while creating a better future for his family.

That goal became the foundation of 1st Choice Plumbing.

From Years of Experience to Building a Family Business

After more than a decade in the plumbing industry, Josue decided it was time to take the next step. He wanted to do more than work a day-to-day job. He wanted to build a business that his family could be proud of, create a living for the people he loves, and use his experience to serve customers directly through a company of his own.

Approximately a year and a half ago, that vision became a reality when 1st Choice Plumbing was established.

Starting a business meant taking the experience Josue had developed over the years and putting it toward something he could build, manage, and grow himself. It meant taking responsibility not only for the work being performed, but also for the relationships built with customers and the reputation of the company.

As a first-generation family business, 1st Choice Plumbing represents the effort to build something lasting through hard work, personal responsibility, and a commitment to providing for the next generation. We understand that a company's reputation is earned one customer at a time, and we approach our work with that responsibility in mind.

A Family Business, From the Work to the Office

What makes 1st Choice Plumbing different is that it is not just a business name. It is a family working together toward a common goal.

Josue leads the plumbing work, bringing more than 10 years of industry experience to the business. His sister, Itzel, plays an important role behind the scenes, managing office responsibilities and helping make sure the administrative side of the business gets handled when it needs to. From keeping things organized to helping the business stay on track, her role helps support the work being done for customers.

Every successful service business needs people who take responsibility and follow through. At 1st Choice Plumbing, family is part of that responsibility. We work together to keep things moving, communicate with customers, and make sure the details behind the service do not get overlooked.

Being family-owned means our name and reputation matter to us personally. We are building a business we want our customers to feel comfortable calling again, recommending to others, and trusting with their plumbing needs.

Our Commitment to Our Customers

We believe customers deserve to work with people they can trust. When someone needs plumbing service, they want to know who they are dealing with, understand what is happening, and feel confident that their concerns are being taken seriously.

That is the standard we strive to uphold at 1st Choice Plumbing.

We are committed to approaching our work with care, communicating with customers, and treating every service call as an opportunity to earn their confidence. We understand that our customers are trusting us with their homes, their properties, and problems that need attention. We take that responsibility seriously.

Our goal is to build lasting relationships through dependable workmanship, professional service, and the kind of personal accountability that comes with running a family business. We do not take a customer's trust for granted. We know it must be earned through the work we do and the way we conduct ourselves.

Proudly Serving Los Angeles County, the Inland Empire, and the West Covina Area

1st Choice Plumbing serves customers throughout Los Angeles County and the Inland Empire, including the West Covina area and surrounding communities. Our goal is to help homeowners and other customers in our service area find a plumbing company they can turn to when they need professional plumbing assistance.

Our service area includes communities across the San Gabriel Valley and nearby parts of Southern California, with service availability depending on the location and the work requested.

We believe in building our business by serving the communities around us, developing relationships with customers, and earning a reputation through the quality of our work. As a family-owned company, we value the opportunity to serve the same communities where families live, work, and build their futures.

For us, serving the community is part of building a business that means something beyond the work itself. Every customer relationship is an opportunity to demonstrate the values on which 1st Choice Plumbing was founded: hard work, responsibility, family, and trust.

Building Something That Lasts

1st Choice Plumbing was built from years of experience and the decision to turn that experience into something of our own. Josue's journey from working in the plumbing industry to establishing a family-owned company reflects the determination to create a better future through hard work and entrepreneurship.

With Josue's plumbing experience, Itzel's support behind the scenes, and a shared commitment to growing the business, we are working toward a future where 1st Choice Plumbing continues to serve customers and build lasting relationships throughout our service area.

We know that trust is not something a company can simply claim. It is something that must be earned, job by job and customer by customer.

At 1st Choice Plumbing, we are a family-owned business built on experience, driven by the future we want to create for our family, and committed to earning the trust of the customers we serve.
