# US location suggestions

City, state, and coordinate suggestions are derived from the GeoNames US postal-code dataset, downloaded October 12, 2026. Postal locality coordinates are approximate; this is not a current municipal-boundary dataset. A city scrape uses a radius around the suggested centre. Whole-state searches use a bounding grid and check business addresses against the selected state; they do not claim complete geographic coverage.

Source: https://download.geonames.org/export/zip/US.zip

GeoNames: https://www.geonames.org/
